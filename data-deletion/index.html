Data Deletion

Effective date: November 23, 2026. Last updated: November 23, 2026.

You can ask ReplayRoot to delete any personal information we hold about you. This page explains how. It also serves as the data deletion instructions for ReplayRoot Publisher on Meta (Instagram), TikTok and Google (YouTube).

Ask us to delete your data

  1. Email hello@replayroot.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Tell us which data to delete. For example, your contact emails, or the account username if you ever connected an account to ReplayRoot Publisher.
  3. We confirm we received your request within 2 business days.
  4. We delete the data within 30 days and email you a confirmation code for your records.

Remove ReplayRoot's access from your account

  • Instagram: open Instagram, go to Settings, then Website permissions, then Apps and websites. Find ReplayRoot Publisher and tap Remove.
  • Facebook: go to Settings and privacy, then Settings, then Apps and websites. Find ReplayRoot Publisher and remove it.
  • TikTok: open TikTok, go to Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions. Find ReplayRoot Publisher and remove access.
  • Google and YouTube: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose ReplayRoot Publisher and select Remove access.

When access is removed, the platform stops ReplayRoot Publisher from using the account. We then delete the stored tokens for that account within 30 days.

Creator clip removal

If a ReplayRoot clip shows you or your content and you want it taken down, email hello@replayroot.com with a link to the clip. We review requests within 2 business days.
