Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026. Last updated: November 23, 2026.

This policy explains what information ReplayRoot collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. ReplayRoot is operated by Uplyft Ventures LLC, a California limited liability company ("ReplayRoot", "we", "us"). It covers the website replayroot.com and ReplayRoot Publisher, the internal tool our team uses to publish clips to our own social media accounts.

1. Who uses ReplayRoot Publisher

ReplayRoot Publisher is used only by the ReplayRoot team to manage accounts that ReplayRoot owns. It does not offer sign-up to the public, and it does not access anyone else's social media account.

3. How we use information

We use the information above only to publish and manage ReplayRoot's own content, measure how it performs, keep our accounts secure, reply to messages and meet legal duties. We do not use platform data to build profiles of individuals, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only:

  • With the platform itself (TikTok, Meta or Google) when we call its API.
  • With service providers that host our website, store our files or run our software, under contracts that limit their use of the data.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect our rights, users or the public.

5. Storage, security and retention

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in a password manager and in encrypted secrets storage. They are never written into source code. Access is limited to the ReplayRoot team, with multi-factor authentication on every account. We keep tokens only while an account is connected and delete them within 30 days of disconnecting. We keep post IDs and performance counts for as long as the post is live and for up to 24 months after, for reporting. Emails are kept for up to 24 months.

6. Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@replayroot.com. Step-by-step deletion instructions are on our Data Deletion page. Creators who want a clip of themselves removed can use the same address.

California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the rights to know, delete and correct personal information and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We will verify your request using the email address you write from, and respond within 45 days.

7. Children

ReplayRoot is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

8. Platform policies

Our use of data from each platform also follows that platform's rules: the TikTok Developer Terms of Service, the Meta Platform Terms, and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service. YouTube data is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke ReplayRoot's access to Google data at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

9. Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the date at the top.
