The best moments in gaming, cut short.

ReplayRoot is a short-form video studio. We find the moments from game streams people want to watch again, then edit them into vertical clips for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. We start with GTA 6.

What we do

Find

Our tools watch trending streams and licensed creator campaigns for moments with real replay value.

Cut

Each moment is cropped to 9:16, captioned word by word and given a clear hook.

Review

A person on our team reviews every clip before it goes live. Nothing posts without approval.

Publish

Approved clips go to the official ReplayRoot accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

Licensed footage only

We clip creators who allow it, mostly through official creator clipping campaigns. We follow each campaign's rules for overlays, sounds, disclosures and credit. If you are a creator and want a clip removed, email us and we will take it down.

How our publishing tool works

ReplayRoot Publisher is the internal tool our team uses to send approved clips to our own ReplayRoot accounts. It connects through each platform's official API and login. On TikTok, the tool sends the finished clip to the ReplayRoot account's inbox as a draft. A team member then opens TikTok, reviews the draft, picks the caption and privacy settings, and posts it. See our Privacy Policy for what data the tool uses.
